## 3. Per-seat positions

**CEO (value & direction).** The owner's ratified identity is research + pipeline-as-asset, with a charter-bounded app vision (`HANDOFF.md` §3, 2026-08-25). Both halves are served by an accepted, disclosed instrument: for the research half, the stopping rule already converts every E2 outcome into a publishable closure, and an instrument with *stated* error is compatible with that — the problem is the word "stated," because the v1.2 teacher's error is currently unstated. For the asset/app half, the labeler *is* the asset, and tonight's evidence is genuinely good on that axis (sentiment cleared with margin; memorization collapsed; zero destroyed features). The CEO's view: accept with conditions, and recognize the real question the owner is answering is "is the alpha question worth ~7–9 overnights read through an instrument sitting exactly on its pre-committed band" — a question the stopping rule was built to make cheap to answer.

**CTO (architecture & simplicity).** The repair worked mostly by clarifying the target, not by improving the model — and that is fine; a clearer rubric is the cheaper, more durable fix. What lazy-elite forbids is chasing the residual: the student's own marginal contribution is +0.0481 with a ceiling visible in the epoch trend (v1.2 e1→e2 bought +3.7 pts exact-set, +0.029 micro F1; the v1.1 record shows epoch 2 bought zero guidance-semantics improvement, `2026-08-22-eval-epoch2/eval_report.md` A.5). Epoch 3, a bigger student, or rubric v1.3 are all frameworks where an extension is needed. The one real engineering item is the **missing→NONE post-rule decision before F4**: 385/6,746 (5.7%) guidance omissions will otherwise be persisted at 100k scale, and A.7 is explicit that adopting the rule at the bench without the writer-side normalization + audit flag misdescribes the produced labels. Accept-with-conditions; the conditions are discipline, not compute.

**CRO — research (methodology).** Three facts dominate. (1) The band is **not cleared** — 0.8078 with CI [0.7503, 0.8528] containing 0.81 means "indistinguishable from the band in either direction," which satisfies the pre-commitment as written ("materially below") but is the weakest possible pass. (2) The pass was achieved substantially by moving the target: the unchanged v1.1 student already sits at 0.7597 [0.6666, 0.8220] against the new teacher, CI also containing the band — had we only relabeled and never retrained, nearly the same acceptance argument would be available. (3) Retention is measured against a teacher whose own error is unmeasured, and the two teachers disagree with each other (0.6481 on the press feature) more than the student disagrees with its teacher — instrument identity is rubric-dependent to a degree that makes "red-flag family" a partly moving target. A hostile reviewer leads with all three. The remedy is not more training (no power argument supports epoch 3); it is **measuring the v1.2 teacher's error before any E2 red-flag number is read**, and per-feature reporting. This seat prefers the spot-check to complete **before F4 launches**, because the decision-theoretic risk is not the $0 machine-hours — it is the rationalization pressure that 9 sunk overnights exert on the subsequent G3 ratification (the exact gate-inversion shape the 2026-08-25 re-evaluation found in this project's own log). A well-bounded null through a measured instrument is a success; the same null through an unmeasured one is a shrug.

**CRO — risk & charter.** No charter exposure in the decision itself (no trading/advice/live capital implicated; F4 is local compute over cached filings). The exposure is honesty and provenance: (i) the v1.1 noise constants traveling in v1.2 manifests is a standing misquote-in-waiting — H3v2 §3.8 hand-off 3 must be executed either way; (ii) "the repair fixed the worst feature" (0.115→0.600) is the kind of sentence that would be written and would be wrong — it was mostly the rubric; (iii) any E2 claim must carry the G3 §1 scope sentence ("detectable through this labeling schema, these features, and this labeler") plus per-feature retention, or the project publishes a number its own record contradicts. Separately: the F4 launch decision (distinct from G1) should not proceed as if F3's corpus were clean — P4's owner-visible items 2–4 (`data/f3/p4_redteam.md` §11: 779 unchecked EXPECTED_ABSENT rows with an estimated 61–179 extractor failures; the garble detector failing its first independent test; four non-PIT gate columns) are open F4 inputs. Accept-with-conditions.

**CFO (cost).** Sunk and to be excluded from the ruling: $24.92 relabel (intro tier; $37.38 standard-rate equivalent), 2 retrain GPU nights, 4.57 h H3v2 relabel, tonight's analysis tokens. Forward costs: **(a) Accept →** F4 W2-core = 9.4 overnights ledger / ~7 at H3v2's measured 1,477.7 chunks/h; $0 cash; near-zero owner attention. **(b) + spot-check →** agent-based (opus subagents, as 2026-08-18): $0 API, heavy subscription-token use, ~2–4 calendar days, and the scarce currency is **owner adjudication attention** (the v1.1 pass required 104 owner rulings; a red-flags-only, base-rate-weighted ~200-chunk design roughly halves that). An API-batch variant needs a new ratification + credit purchase — strictly dominated by the agent path. **(c) Repairs →** epoch 3: 1 GPU night + ~1 h eval + 4.6 h H3v3 + quant re-derivation, expected family gain small against the +0.048 student-arm evidence; rubric v1.3: ~$25–37 API (needs credits + ratification) + 2 nights + full re-measure, and a *second* target move destroys cross-campaign comparability — poor EV, named as such. **(d) Hold →** calendar only. The CFO accepts, prefers not serializing ~7–9 overnights of free machine time behind a measurement whose principal value is for the final report — but concedes the CRO-research point that the pre-commitment in §7 below must be ratified *now* precisely so concurrency cannot rot into rationalization.

## 4. The strongest case AGAINST accept (steelman)

The kill-band was ρ≈0.81 and the instrument sits **on** it — 0.0022 below, CI straddling — after the target was moved to meet it. The 2×2 is unambiguous about who moved: the rubric, model held fixed, is worth +0.0904; the student, teacher held fixed, +0.0481; the press-rate recovery is *entirely* the rubric and MARGIN_mda *mostly* — for that category the student's actual chunk-level recall **fell** (0.746→0.681) while the rubric added 294 rows of it. At the chunk level, the repaired student is *worse* on both structured fields (exact-set −0.018, guidance −0.067, omissions 321→385). What improved is Pearson over filing-level rates — a statistic blind to constant bias — against a teacher whose own error nobody has measured, and the two rubrics' teachers agree with each other at only 0.6481 on the press feature, worse than the student agrees with either. So E2's alpha question would be read through: truth → v1.2 teacher (error unknown; the v1.1 analogue was ~25% exact-set on the representative tier) → student (family 0.81, worst member 0.42) → 22 rate features. Attenuation multiplies down the achievable IC while H2's honest MDE is already 0.032–0.085; a null result through this chain is not "no signal," it is "no signal *or* signal destroyed in the chain, indistinguishable," which is a weaker closure than the stopping rule promises the owner. Nine overnights and the F5/F6 sessions are cheap in cash and expensive in the only currencies this project actually spends — owner attention and calendar — and they purchase a result whose headline caveat is currently unmeasurable. If the alpha question deserves answering, it deserves an instrument whose noise is measured first; if it does not, F4 should not run at all.

## 5. The strongest case FOR accept

The ratified pre-commitment (H5 §5 item 2) asked one question — is retention *materially below* the ρ≈0.57/0.81 band — and the answer flipped from yes (v1.1: CI excluded 0.81) to no (v1.2: CI contains it), with sentiment now clearing its band entirely from below and **zero** destroyed features against two. The memorization penalty collapsed 0.174→0.037, meaning the eval-slice number is finally an honest estimate of behavior on unseen text — which is what F4 is. The "it was mostly the rubric" objection cuts less than it seems: E2 will be labeled *by this student against this rubric's construct*; the relevant configuration is exactly the bottom-right 2×2 cell, and in it the student's contribution is positive and distinguishable from zero (+0.0481 [+0.0096, +0.1015]) with the clean win landing on the feature H3 singled out as weakest (`sentiment_negative_share`, +0.1566). Instrument error does not need to be zero — it needs to be fixed, disclosed, and consistently applied, and G3's skeleton already pre-registers exactly that (§1 scope sentence, §11 instrument disclosures, per-feature retention hand-off). Attenuation biases *toward* the null — against the charter's worst outcome, a biased positive. The alternative repairs have measurably poor expected value (§3-CFO), the stopping rule caps every downstream branch, and the marginal cash cost of proceeding is $0. Holding a $0, machine-hours-only campaign hostage to a second-rater pass whose result changes the *report's caveats* more than the *go decision* is the kind of process perfectionism the lazy-elite mandate exists to prevent.

## 6. Consolidated recommendation, with named dissents

**ACCEPT-WITH-CONDITIONS (Option B below), 5 seats concurring on the ruling, split 3–2 on sequencing.**

The conditions, precisely:

1. **Order a v1.2 spot-check second-rater pass now, agent-based, $0 API** — red-flags-focused, base-rate-representative, ~200 chunks (the v1.1 Tier-C arm at n=36 gave CI [13.8, 41.1] on error, too wide to carry a gate; n≈200 gives roughly ±6–7 pts). Its purpose: put a measured error on the v1.2 teacher so H3v2's retention numbers have a denominator. **Hard condition regardless of sequencing: it completes before the G3 pre-registration is ratified and before any E2 IC or red-flag result is read** (this dovetails with kill-criterion 6's existing no-IC-before-G3 freeze).
2. **Per-feature retention reporting in E2, never a family mean** (H3 §3.5 item 3, H3v2 §3.8 item 1 — already handed to G3). The two weakest members (DEMAND_mda 0.4238, MARGIN_mda 0.6000) travel with their CIs wherever red-flag features appear. Any *exclusion or down-weighting* of weak features is done only if pre-registered in G3 as a named sensitivity row — deciding it tonight, after seeing retention, would itself be post-hoc selection.
3. **Any cross-rubric claim ships its teacher-arm control** (H3v2 §3.8 item 2) — the sentence "the repair worked" is never written without the +0.0904/+0.0481 split beside it.
4. **The v1.1 noise constants stop traveling with v1.2 artifacts** (H3v2 §3.8 item 3) — until condition 1 lands, the honest annotation on every v1.2 red-flag number is "teacher error unmeasured."
5. **The missing→NONE post-rule is decided by the owner before F4 launches**, and if adopted, adopted at the labeling writer with the audit flag per `2026-08-22-eval-epoch2/eval_report.md` A.7 — with the disclosure that the underlying omission rate worsened (321→385) and `guidance_signed_mean` pooled retention regressed to 0.6248 [0.3271, 0.8776].

**Sequencing split, recorded as genuine dissent:**
- **CFO, CEO, CTO (majority):** F4 may launch on acceptance; the spot-check runs concurrently on the agent lane. Rationale: F4 is $0 and machine-hours; the pre-commitments in §7 are ratified now, which is what removes the rationalization risk.
- **CRO-research, CRO-risk (dissent):** the spot-check completes **before** F4's first overnight. Rationale: the project's own decision log contains one gate-inversion already; pre-commitments on paper are weaker than sequencing in fact, and the cost of waiting is ~2–4 calendar days against a 9-overnight campaign.

**Unanimous:** Option C (further repair) is not recommended — no arm of the evidence says epoch 3, rubric v1.3, or a bigger student moves family retention from "on the band" to "clears the band" at acceptable cost, and a second rubric move would destroy comparability.

## 7. Kill-criteria / pre-commitments — stated NOW, so future selves cannot rationalize past them

1. **If the v1.2 spot-check measures teacher red-flag exact-set error materially worse than v1.1's representative estimate** (operationally: Wilson CI lower bound on error above ~35% on the base-rate-representative draw), the E2 red-flag feature family is demoted to exploratory/disclosure-only in G3 — **regardless of how many F4 overnights are already sunk.** Sentiment and composition features are unaffected.
2. **If the spot-check cannot run** (owner declines the attention cost), then E2's red-flag results carry "teacher error unmeasured under rubric v1.2" as a first-class limitation in every table where they appear — not a footnote — and no red-flag-driven headline claim is made.
3. **If F4 labeling shows behavior the smoke never showed** — `finish=length` runs, parse-failure rate above 0 sustained, or per-section agreement drift beyond the H3v2 manifest's bands — stop and report, per the standing H3v2 §1.4 rule; do not label through it.
4. **If E2's measured folds put the honest MDE above what a ~0.81-retention instrument can plausibly reach** (kill-criterion 3's machinery, now with attenuation known), the margin/design re-cut happens by explicit ratification before any delta is read — never a silent reinterpretation.
5. **No epoch 3 and no rubric v1.3 without a new owner ratification** naming what changed and its own expected gain — the burden of proof set by the +0.0481 student-arm measurement.
6. Existing ratified pre-commitments carry unchanged: H5 kill-criteria 1–6, the stopping rule (all three E2 outcomes close the alpha question), the spend freeze (re-sealed; ratification + credits required for any API call).

1. **Sunk cost, by name:** $24.92 of relabel spend, 2 retrain nights, and the repair narrative itself. The project's own 2026-08-25 re-evaluation found "the canonical sunk-cost signature in our own decision log": the fine-tune gate *"('only if Week 5 shows real signal') inverted into the reason to expand when Week 5 showed none"* (`REEVALUATION_2026-08-25.md` §2). The analogous shape tonight is "we repaired it, therefore it must pass." The 2×2 exists to block exactly this: the repair narrative is about one-third true.
2. **The headline Δ +0.1385 read as student improvement** — ~⅔ is the target moving (H3v2 §3.4).
3. **The pooled 0.8721** — 85% memorized, an upper bound, never clears a gate (H3v2 §3.7 caveat 1).
4. **The 98.4% post-ruled guidance figure** — majority-class-carried; 11/17 on real guidance rows (`2026-08-22-eval-epoch2/eval_report.md` A.3).
5. **The v1.1 teacher-noise constants** (36.6%/25%/7.5%) quoted as v1.2 properties — they are not (H3v2 §3.7 caveat 3).
6. **F3/P4 corpus findings leaking into the labeler judgment** — P4's items are F4-*launch* inputs (corpus QA), not G1 inputs; conversely, ruling G1 "accept" does not clear them.
7. **This brief itself** — model counsel, weighed by the owner, never recorded as the owner's judgment (HANDOFF §7 provenance rule).
