**CRO — research (methodology).** The branches must be defined by a *named procedure*, not adjudicated by feel: TOST (or explicit 95% CI containment) at a stated margin, block-bootstrap/Newey-West SE, honest MDE from H2 (~0.029 bootstrap-anchor / ~0.039 at ρ_f=0.3 / ~0.036–0.049 true-construct — not §2a's stale 0.019–0.037), and the corrected scope sentence ("detectable through this schema, these features, this labeler" — never "exists in this universe"). I dissent from any escape valve on ambiguity (CFO's Option 2): E1's guidance family (+0.0345, 5/6 folds, flipping to −0.0125 under form control) is proof this project can generate ambiguity that *feels* like signal, and a valve that exists gets used. Strict Option 1.

**CRO — risk & charter.** Three points. (i) The positive branch is the charter-critical one: an above-MDE effect's pre-committed response must be robustness-then-write-up-then-stop — the charter forbids the only action a positive implies, the write-up must contain no "beats the market" language, and the owner's recorded "help with my personal investments" vision sits close enough to the line that the rule should restate: a positive is a research finding, never a signal to act on. (ii) Any published positive rests on a price source never ratified (Yahoo, robots.txt-disallowed, inherited from a fallback — decision_audit §1.11); the G3 table must force that ratification before any head runs. (iii) Close the mid-cap ramp honestly rather than absolutely: as an alpha vehicle it is a pre-built E3 ramp and should be shut; but large caps structurally contain one bankruptcy (alternatives D2a), so a *distress* project would legitimately need mid-caps — name that reopen path inside the E3 policy instead of pretending the ramp is dead. Provenance: this whole document is model counsel until signed.

**CFO (cost).** What the rule is worth: E3 at E2's observed unit costs is ~5–9 weeks of owner attention, 10–20 machine overnights, a second full owner-adjudicated spot-check, and the most token-expensive phases repeated — against a one-page rule costing one owner read. The named sunk-cost pattern this rule exists to prevent has already occurred once: the fine-tune gate ("only if Week 5 shows signal") inverted into the reason to expand (kill_case S1). My one disagreement: pre-price the ambiguous-branch instrument levers NOW — $16.09 full re-label at measured rates, ~1 night for a third epoch, ~0 marginal cost for extra folds from already-ingested data — and attach the price list to the rule as *information*, because the alternative is that a future session re-derives them under temptation. I accept the CRO-research position that no lever is pre-*authorized*; I want them pre-*priced*. That is Option 1 with the price annex, which is how the consolidated recommendation resolves it.

## 4. The strongest case AGAINST the recommendation

You are being asked to pre-commit to never re-asking a question *while the instrument that will answer it is known to be broken and the repair costs $16*. H3 has not landed. If it shows what the simulation predicts — `sentiment_negative_share` retaining ρ≈0.57, NEGATIVE recall 0.487, the one feature most likely to carry signal half-erased — then an ambiguous E2 result is substantially an *instrument* fact, not a *world* fact, and Option 1 pre-commits you to publish "the question is closed" when the honest sentence is "our ruler was bent and we ratified a rule forbidding us to buy a straight one." The re-label that fixes the training targets costs $16.09; a third epoch costs one night; the rule makes both feel like violations. Second: the decision this rule pathologizes — null → diagnose → bigger experiment — was graded SOUND by every lens when it happened (decision_audit §1.12–1.13). E1→E2 was not a hole; it was correct science. A rule engineered to prevent the last good decision from recurring will, in the branch where E2 is ambiguous *for a diagnosable, repairable reason*, prevent a good decision again — and the "new ratification naming what changed" escape hatch is cold comfort, because the whole psychological force of a pre-commitment device is that future selves treat invoking the hatch as failure. Third: the three-outcome taxonomy is being frozen before H1's positive controls report; if T1/T2 fail, every branch is void and you ratified ceremony. The honest rebuttal — the E3 policy *is* the diagnosed-repair path, requiring only that the change be named in writing — is real, but do not pretend the rule is free: it deliberately raises the cost of being right a second time in order to lower the cost of being wrong forever.

## 5. Kill-criteria / pre-commitments (stated now, so future selves cannot rationalize past them)

1. **If H1's positive controls fail** (T1 shows no announcement-return/surprise relation, or T2 recovers no documented anomaly), the three-branch taxonomy is VOID — E2's outcomes would be uninterpretable plumbing, not answers. The stopping rule must be re-drafted, not mechanically carried.
2. **If H3 shows the student-labeled E1 backtest decorrelates the text features beyond usability** (feature retention or IC-delta correspondence materially below the lens simulation's ρ≈0.57/0.81 band, threshold to be fixed at the G1 ruling), G1 should fail and this rule must be re-ratified against a *repaired* instrument (third epoch / $16 re-label decision) before F3 — the branches as drafted assume the instrument G1 accepts.
3. **If H2's honest measured-unit MDE lands above ~0.05**, the ±0.03 equivalence margin is unreachable, the "bounded null" branch cannot trigger as written, and the margin or the design must be re-cut by explicit ratification — never silently reinterpreted.
4. **If measured fold autocorrelation at E2 substantially exceeds the assumed ρ_f=0.3**, publish the degraded bound; do not re-run or re-window to recover the headline.
5. **If the owner ratifies distribution or a product pivot** (identity change per HANDOFF §3), re-convene: the branches change meaning.
6. **Freeze discipline:** no session computes or reads any E2 IC before the instantiated G3 document is owner-ratified and sha-pinned; any amendment after the first IC exists is labeled POST-HOC in the document itself.
