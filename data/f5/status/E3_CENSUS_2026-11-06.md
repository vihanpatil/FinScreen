## D. The honest MDE (`section_4_mde`) — mirrors census_e2 §2, with level-IC anchors

The proposed primary metric is the **mean per-fold dedup Spearman IC of a no-fit, sign-fixed
family composite against `target_excess_63`** — a *level* IC, not E2's text-minus-numeric
*delta*. census_e2's delta anchor (H2's within-fold bootstrap SD of a difference between two
fitted blocks that share the numeric features) does not transfer: a level IC has no shared
component to cancel (`.algebra.why_not_the_h2_delta_anchor`). So the anchors are the
cross-fold SDs of single-predictor level ICs **already measured on this universe and read
from committed files** — never computed here (`.anchors.*.source`):

| anchor | cross-fold SD of per-fold IC | k | measured at (rows per fold) | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E2 zero-information size rank (dedup) | **0.1543** | 33 | n_dd 135.79 (frame B) | `results_head1.json .standing.zero_information_summary[size_only_rank].std_ic_dedup` |
| E2 ticker-training-mean (dedup) | 0.2016 | 33 | 135.79 | same, `ticker_training_mean_rank` |
| H1 T2a PEAD (SUE at earnings 8-Ks, dedup) | 0.1604 | 40 | 84.2 events/quarter | `controls_results.json .controls.T2a_pead_core.dedup.sd_ic_ddof1` |
| H1 T2b 12-1 momentum (earnings 8-Ks, dedup) | 0.2484 | 40 | 97.0 | same, `T2b_momentum_core` |

Algebra (`.algebra`): SE = SD/√33 × AR(1) inflation (ρ = 0, or the H2-assumed 0.3 → ×1.3491);
MDE = 2.8 × SE for the single primary test; Holm worst case over the 4 family secondaries =
(z₀.₉₉₃₇₅ + z₀.₈₀) × SE = 3.339 × SE; margin Δ = smallest rung of **G3's ladder {0.03, 0.04,
0.05}** with SE ≤ Δ/2.487 (caps 0.0121 / 0.0161 / 0.0201), none → UNPOWERED. **The two E2
anchors were measured at frame B's per-fold size; the `*_n_adjusted_to_this_frame` rows swap
the 1/(n−1) sampling component to each frame's own n_dd** (red-team S11: the first draft said
the anchors do not depend on the frame, which is wrong for that component). The H1 anchors
were measured at 84–97 events a quarter, close to A's 92.5, and are not adjusted.

The H1 zero-information floor — the |IC| a zero-information predictor reaches on H1's event
panel — is **0.0466–0.0488** (`.h1_zero_information_floor`, 2.8 × the T0 placebo SE; the
analogous pure-noise MDE on A's own per-fold size is 0.051 — reviewer N7; the
pre-registration's positive branch uses the minimum over the two T0 arms).

**What this says, plainly.** Measured single-predictor level ICs on this universe disperse
across quarters with SD 0.15–0.25, so one family needs a true mean |IC| of about **0.07 to
0.16** to be detected at 80% power over 33 folds, and **no rung of the inherited ladder is
reachable at any measured anchor** (only the independence lower bound at ρ = 0 reaches 0.05).
A documented factor's IC in a ~100-name large-cap universe is typically a fraction of that.
**The design is therefore expected to come back UNPOWERED**, and the pre-registration says so
in §1 and §13; an earlier draft widened the ladder to 0.10, which would have relabelled that
outcome a "bounded null", and was withdrawn (red-team M3). The only route to a smaller SE is
the 4-family composite (averaging may shrink per-fold dispersion), and no measurement of its
dispersion exists before the run, so no smaller anchor is claimed (`.algebra.caveats`). Every
MDE is a lower bound (nested folds, overlapping windows); the t-correction at k = 33 (2.890 vs
2.8) is not applied, matching census_e2.
