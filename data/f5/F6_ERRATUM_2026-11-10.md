# Erratum to the E2 result (`F6_E2_RESULT.md`) — 2026-11-10

`F6_E2_RESULT.md` is the write-up the owner accepted at Gate G4 and is left byte-identical. This file corrects
the record beside it. Everything measured here is **POST-HOC: executed after the primary was seen, never selectable
as the primary. The ratified G3 result stands and no §13 branch is re-labelled.** Authorised by the owner's words
"Run it" (HANDOFF §3, 2026-11-10).

## 1. The defect

`numeric_features_e2.py:844` computes `book_to_market = equity / (shares × close before the filing)`. The share
count is the one the company had reported at the time; the close comes from a Yahoo series fetched 2026-08-24,
which the source adjusts backwards for every later stock split and spin-off. So:

- **Look-ahead part.** For a filing made before such an event, the feature is too large by the adjustment factor
  and reflects an event that was not public at the filing date.
- **Stale part (not look-ahead).** For a filing after a split takes effect but before the next reported share
  count, a pre-split count meets a post-split close, with the same inflation. 2 scored rows (reviewer's count).

Both fitted blocks (`numeric_only`, `text_and_numeric`) were trained and evaluated with the feature. Three
red-team passes before ratification missed it: they checked dates, and the leak is in the units of a vendor series.

**Reach** (`sensitivity_split_lookahead_seeds.json`, `factor_reach`): of the 4,481 scored deduplicated rows, **417
rows in 18 companies** carry an adjustment factor other than 1 with a non-null feature. The earlier share-count
census (`split_lookahead_census.json`: 251 rows, 11 companies) sees only changes of 1.8× or more that are visible
in the stored share counts; it misses spin-off adjustments (factors about 1.03 to 1.32) and two splits whose stored
rows end before the split, and it includes one row that is a reporting error rather than a split.

## 3. What it means

1. **The defect was real and inflated the measured level of both fitted blocks by about 0.010 to 0.013 IC**,
   roughly half of the numeric block's seed-mean level. No level, before or after, clears the noise floor the
   write-up already states (§1.7).
2. **It inflated both about equally, so the quantity E2 tested moved by about +0.002.** The §13 outcome, "text is
   not used as a signal", is unaffected. The equivalence label was already seed-dependent (the write-up says so);
   under the corrected feature it is somewhat more so, and with the feature removed it is reached at fewer than
   half the seeds, mostly because the procedure returns UNPOWERED more often. In both branches §13 prescribes the
   same consequence.
3. **A third of the per-filing calls depend on the model seed alone.** Refitting the ratified model with only its
   seed changed alters 1,324 to 1,469 of the 4,481 calls in the `text_and_numeric` column (median 1,412). The
   per-company scorecard rows are therefore illustrative, not a stable record; the scorecard carried no seed
   disclosure until 2026-11-10.

## 4. A reading that was wrong, kept on the record

The arm was first run at seed 0 only (`sensitivity_split_lookahead.json`): delta +0.0011 ratified, +0.0310 arm B,
+0.0218 arm A. The rule fixed before that run compared those with twice the ratified seed-band sd (0.0193) and so
labelled both "moved beyond seed noise". **That label is not a finding about the defect.** It compares one seed
with one seed: the ratified seed 0 sits at the 16th percentile of its own band, and 20 of the 100 ratified seeds
differ from it by more than that bound with no feature change at all. The main session showed the owner the seed-0
table before the reviewer's pass; it should not have. The seed-matched table in §2 is the measurement.

## 5. Consequences for other work

- The numeric-families program (E3, deferred by the owner 2026-11-09) must not be re-opened on the stored
  `book_to_market` column: its draft "value" family is that column, and the level inflation matters more there
  than for the text question.
- Any forward use of the numeric block needs the feature computed on one basis (share count and price both as of
  the day). The withdrawn P5 packet waits on that.
