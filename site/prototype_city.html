01 · Source

Straight from EDGAR.

05 · Read

A reader that runs on a laptop.

NEGATIVEnet income decreased 37 percent GUIDANCE · NONEno direction versus prior guidance MARGIN PRESSUREexploratory
A 7-billion-parameter model reads every passage: tone, guidance direction, risk flags. It cost $0 to run. It is wrong a measured share of the time, and that share is printed on every report: sentiment agreement 86.9%, guidance 96.6% on the no-guidance mass, risk flags 28% error, exploratory only.

06 · Time

Reports land after the close.

accepted 16:30 ETnext open 09:3063 sessions

The tool only ever knows what was public at that minute. 45% of filings arrive after 4 pm; the first session that could react is the next morning. This is where most backtests cheat, and where this one does not.

08 · Test

Does any of it predict what happens next?

The wall is time. Everything behind it is known; everything ahead is future. The model is trained only on the past and scored only on what came after, quarter by quarter, rules frozen before the data.

So far the honest answer: not yet proven. The first study on 25 companies found no robust text signal. The larger study is frozen until its rules are ratified, and its result gets published either way.
